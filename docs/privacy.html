The Zero-Storage Guarantee at a Glance
Trouvé DocDue does not operate an application server, cloud database, user account registry, or tracking SDK. All OCR processing and document detection run strictly on your physical device. Your photos and personal document contents are never transmitted across the network or stored apart from your device.

1. Architecture & Core Philosophy

Trouvé DocDue ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed from inception with a local-first, privacy-by-design model. We believe that your personal identity and financial documents—such as passports, driving licences, vehicle registrations, and insurance policies—should remain exclusively in your possession.

The app operates entirely offline. It does not require account creation, email registration, login credentials, or cloud synchronization.

2. Gallery Inspection & 100% On-Device OCR

When you choose to discover documents from your device photo library:

Explicit User Consent: Discovery scanning is only initiated after you explicitly review discovery modes and provide consent.
Local Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Text recognition and classification execute 100% on-device using Google ML Kit on Android and native on-device libraries. No image frames, raw pixels, or recognized text strings are sent to any remote server or third-party AI platform.
Immediate Memory Release: Photos determined not to be relevant documents (e.g., family pictures, landscapes, non-document screenshots) are purged from volatile memory immediately after classification.
Read-Only Gallery Access: Your photo library is treated as strictly read-only. Trouvé DocDue never deletes, modifies, renames, moves, or overwrites your original photos.

3. Document Detection Targets

Our on-device classification models are trained to locate high-priority documents with renewal or expiration timelines:

Passports & Visas: MRZ codes, passport numbers, issuing authority, and expiry dates.
Driving Licences: Licence identifiers, validity/expiry dates, vehicle authorization classes.
Insurance Policies & Certificates: Motor insurance schedules, health insurance cards, life insurance commencement/maturity dates, policy numbers, and renewal due dates.
Vehicle Documentation: Registration certificates (RC), Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificates, and transport permits.

4. Consent-Based Document Vault

Scanning your library does not automatically add records to your app vault:

Review Queue: Detected documents are presented in an uncommitted review queue where you can inspect extracted dates, correct titles, and verify field values.
Approval Gate: Only documents you explicitly approve are saved into the app's local SQLite database.
Rejection & Deletion: Any candidate you reject is immediately discarded from the temporary cache with no residual trace.

5. Device Permissions & Geolocation Stance

Trouvé DocDue requests only the minimal permissions necessary to perform its functions:

Photo Library Permission: Required solely to read photos during a scan. On Android 13+, granular photo permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) are utilized.
Notification Permission: Required to deliver local notifications on your device prior to a document's expiry or renewal date.
Zero Geolocation Tracking: We intentionally do not request or use ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION. The app does not track where your photos were taken or record your geographic coordinates.

6. Local Notification Reminders

All alerts, expiry alarms, and renewal reminders are scheduled directly through your mobile device's local operating system alarm and notification framework (e.g. Android NotificationManager / iOS UserNotifications). We do not register device push tokens, and no notification payloads pass through remote push servers.

7. Data Ownership, Portability & Complete Erasure

Because your data lives only on your device, you have complete sovereignty:

Data Export: You can export all saved document records and reminder metadata at any time in structured JSON format via the Settings menu.
Purge Cache: You can instantly clear uncommitted candidate results with a single tap.
Complete Data Erasure: You can permanently wipe all records, local databases, attachments, and settings directly from the app's Settings screen. Uninstalling the app completely removes all data stored by Trouvé DocDue.

8. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

Trouvé DocDue contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero behavioral trackers, and zero analytics profiling. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share user data under any circumstances.
