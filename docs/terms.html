2. Description of the Service

Trouvé DocDue is an offline personal productivity utility designed to discover personal documents (such as passports, driving licences, motor/health/life insurance policies, and vehicle registrations) that you already have in your device's photo gallery, extract dates and metadata locally, and schedule local expiry and renewal alarms.

The App performs all text recognition and document classification locally on your device hardware without transmitting your documents to the cloud.

3. OCR Accuracy & User Verification Disclaimer

While Trouvé DocDue employs advanced on-device ML models, recognition accuracy can be impacted by image blur, lighting, fold creases, non-standard typography, low resolution, or partial obstructions.
