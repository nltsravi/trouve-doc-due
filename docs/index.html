Effortless Gallery Discovery
Discovers important personal documents already resting in your photo camera roll so you never have to manually photograph or enter them one by one.
⚡
100% On-Device OCR
Powered by local Google ML Kit machine learning. Text recognition and date parsing run locally on your phone hardware. Zero cloud APIs.
🛡️
Zero-Storage Guarantee
Photos and personal documents are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party database. Non-document images are immediately purged from volatile RAM.
⏰
Smart Local Reminders
Schedules proactive notifications (30 days, 7 days, 1 day before expiry) using your device's native notification system without remote push servers.
🗂️
Target Document Support
Intelligently identifies Passports (MRZ), Driving Licences, Motor/Health/Life Insurance policies, Vehicle Registration (RC), and PUC certificates.
👤
Consent-First Review Vault
Nothing is saved into the database without your explicit inspection and approval. You stay in complete control of your records at all times.
